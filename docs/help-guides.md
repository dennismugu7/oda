# Help guides: drafts for the owner's review (M8b)

First drafts, written in M8b (decision #74 f) for the owner to review before they're bundled in
the app's Help screen (20b-help, CP3). Plain English, short. Each guide has a title, a one-line
summary (shown in the list and used by search) and a few short sections. The search also
matches the **keywords** line, which buyers never see. Contact: email only (decision #77).

---

## 1. Getting paid

**Summary:** Buyers pay you directly on M-Pesa. oda never touches your money.
**Keywords:** M-Pesa, till, pochi, paybill, payment, confirm, refund, money, pay on delivery

**How buyers pay you.** Add your M-Pesa account in **More → Payments**: a Buy Goods Till, Pochi la Biashara or a Paybill. At checkout, buyers see your number and the name on the account, and pay you straight from their phone.

**Before delivery or on delivery.** In **Payments → When buyers pay**, choose **Before delivery** (the buyer pays when ordering), **On delivery** (the buyer pays you or your rider when the order arrives), or both. At least one stays on.

**When a buyer says they paid.** The order shows **Says paid** with their M-Pesa code. Check your M-Pesa messages for that code and amount, then tap **Confirm payment**. If the money didn't arrive, don't confirm: message the buyer from the order.

**Unpaid orders.** If a buyer doesn't pay within 48 hours, the order is cancelled for you and the buyer gets an email.

**Refunds.** If you cancel an order the buyer already paid for, it shows **Refund due** in your orders. Send the money back on M-Pesa yourself, then tap **Mark refund sent**. The buyer gets an email.

## 2. Delivery and pickup

**Summary:** Set where you deliver, what it costs, and whether buyers can pick up.
**Keywords:** delivery, rider, parcel, pickup, fee, free delivery, area, location, send, sent

**Delivery areas.** In **More → Delivery**, add each place you deliver to, with its fee, how it travels (rider or parcel) and how long it takes (for example "Same day"). Buyers pick one at checkout and the fee is added to their total.

**Pickup.** Turn on **Buyers can pick up** and say where and when. Pickup is free for the buyer.

**Free delivery.** Set an amount, for example KSh 5,000, and mark the areas it applies to. Orders over that amount get free delivery in those areas.

**You need one way to deliver.** Without a delivery area or pickup, your shop shows "Not taking orders yet".

**Sending an order.** When it's on its way, open the order and tap **Mark as sent**. When the buyer has it, tap **Mark as delivered**. The buyer gets an update each time.

## 3. Your orders

**Summary:** Accept, confirm, send and deliver orders, or add one you took on WhatsApp.
**Keywords:** order, accept, confirm, cancel, sent, delivered, new order, manual order, WhatsApp, customer

**New orders.** You get a notification for each new order. Pay-on-delivery orders wait for you to tap **Accept**. Pay-now orders wait for the buyer to pay, then for you to **Confirm payment**.

**The order steps.** Accepted or paid → **Mark as sent** → **Mark as delivered**. The **Orders** tab groups what needs you first.

**Talk to the buyer.** Every order has a WhatsApp button with a ready message (the order number and link). Edit it before you send.

**Cancelling.** Open the order, tap **Cancel order** and pick a reason. The buyer gets an email. If they had paid, you refund them (see *Getting paid*).

**Orders from DMs and calls.** Tap **New order** on the Orders tab to record an order you took on WhatsApp, TikTok or in person. It counts in your sales and customers like any other.

## 4. Reviews

**Summary:** Buyers rate delivered orders. You can reply, or report a review that breaks the rules.
**Keywords:** review, rating, stars, reply, report, photo, feedback from buyers

**Who can review.** Only a buyer whose order was delivered, once per order. They can edit their review for 7 days. Orders you added yourself can't be reviewed.

**Replying.** Open **More → Reviews**, tap a review and write a reply. Buyers see it under the review on your product page.

**Reporting.** If a review is fake, rude or shares private details, tap **Report**. Its photos are hidden at once, and the oda team checks it. The stars still count until the team decides.

**Your rating.** Your shop shows the average stars and the number of reviews. Each product shows its own rating from the items buyers rated.

## 5. Sharing your shop

**Summary:** Share your link everywhere you sell, and see which places bring orders.
**Keywords:** share, link, QR code, TikTok, Instagram, WhatsApp, Status, Facebook, caption, insights

**Your link.** Your shop lives at your link (see **More → Shop profile**). Put it in your TikTok and Instagram bio, your WhatsApp Status and your chats.

**Share from oda.** Use **Share** to post a ready image (your newest product, your shop, or a QR code) with a caption in English or Swahili. Links shared from oda are tagged, so **Insights** can show where your orders come from.

**QR code.** Print it or show it at your stall: buyers scan it with their camera to open your shop.

**Changed your link?** Your old link keeps working and sends buyers to the new one.

## 6. Your account and getting help

**Summary:** Your details, password, pausing your shop, deleting your account, and how to reach us.
**Keywords:** account, password, email, phone, pause, break, holiday, delete, close shop, support, help, contact

**Your details and password.** In **More → Settings** you can change your name, your recovery phone and your password. Your email can't be changed yet.

**Taking a break.** Turn on **Pause my shop**. Buyers can still look around but can't order until you turn it off.

**Deleting your account.** **Settings → Delete account**, then type DELETE. Your shop closes at once. Your products, orders, customers and photos are removed after 30 days. This can't be undone. You can also do this on the web at your oda link's /delete-account page.

**Getting help.** Email **support@mugu-labs.com**. To suggest an idea or report a problem, use **Settings → Share feedback** (you can add a screenshot). We read every message.

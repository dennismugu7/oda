# TikTok logo: permission request (draft for the owner to send)

Decision #101 (c): oda shows a neutral icon for TikTok until TikTok approves in writing (#55).
If they approve, record the approval in `docs/decisions.md` and switch to the official logo
from TikTok's brand pack, unmodified, following their rules.

**To:** TikTok's brand team, through the contact form or address on TikTok's brand guidelines
page (brand.tiktok.com / TikTok for Developers → Brand guidelines). Use the address they list;
don't guess one.

**Subject:** Permission to use the TikTok logo in oda, a free shop app for Kenyan sellers

Hello TikTok Brand team,

I run oda (https://mugu-labs.com/products/oda/), a free Android app from Mugu Labs, Kenya,
that gives small social sellers a shop link they can share with their customers.

Many of our sellers sell through TikTok. We would like to use the official TikTok logo, unmodified
and following your brand guidelines, in two places in the app only:

1. A "Share to TikTok" button that copies the seller's shop link so they can add it to their
   TikTok profile or posts (we do not post to TikTok on their behalf and do not use the TikTok API).
2. An onboarding question, "Where do you sell today?", where a seller can tick "TikTok" among
   other channels such as WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook.

The logo would always appear next to the word "TikTok", never as oda's own branding, and would
not suggest any partnership with or endorsement by TikTok.

Could you let us know whether this use is allowed, and which logo files and rules we should
follow? Until we hear from you, we use a neutral icon.
